## Original artwork (no credit needed)

The SVG illustrations in `art/` (honeycomb pattern, bee, hive, pipeline diagram) and the
placeholder SVGs in `placeholders/` were created for this site and are original work.
They may be reused with the site only once the ownership/licensing decision (local
open-items list, IP-1) is recorded. Until then, do not assume reuse is allowed.

## What is NOT used

No logos (SAWiE, NARC, HBRI, Namal, organisers), no photos of identifiable people, no
event or competition photos, no project poster, and nothing copied from other websites
without a free licence. See the Image credits page on the site.
